## What a wheel speed sensor code means

Every wheel has a speed sensor that reads a toothed ring, called the tone
ring, as the wheel turns. The ABS computer compares all four wheels many times
a second. A wheel slowing much faster than the others means it is about to
lock, and the ABS releases that brake for a moment.

C0035 names the left front sensor and C0040 the right front. When one sensor
stops giving a believable signal, the computer cannot tell whether that wheel
is locking, so it switches ABS and stability control off and lights the ABS
warning lamp.

## What it means for driving

The ordinary brakes still work exactly as before. What is lost is the
protection in an emergency stop: on a wet road the wheels can lock, the car
can skid, and steering while braking hard stops working. On Sri Lankan roads
in monsoon rain that is a real risk, so the car should be booked in soon and
driven gently with more following distance until it is.

## How the mechanic finds the cause

A diagnostic scanner that reads ABS live data shows the speed reported by all
four wheels while the car rolls. The faulty wheel reads zero, drops out, or
jumps around while the others agree.

The mechanic then checks the wiring and connector at that wheel, which run
close to the suspension and are often damaged by water, mud, or a stone. The
sensor tip is checked for metal dust and the tone ring for cracked or missing
teeth or heavy rust. The sensor's own resistance or output can be measured
with a multimeter or oscilloscope.

## The usual repairs

Often the fix is cleaning a sensor tip coated in brake dust and metal debris
or repairing a corroded connector, which is cheap. A sensor that has failed
internally is unbolted and replaced. A damaged tone ring is replaced, which
on some cars means replacing the hub bearing it is part of.

After the repair the fault code is cleared and the car is test driven to
confirm the ABS lamp stays off and all four wheel speeds agree.

## How long the repair takes

Diagnosis takes about half an hour. Replacing a sensor takes thirty minutes to
an hour per wheel. Replacing a hub bearing with an integrated tone ring takes
one to two hours per wheel.

## What to check afterwards

The ABS warning lamp must go out after the engine is started and stay out
after a drive. Ask whether the sensor was replaced or only cleaned, and which
wheel was worked on. If the brake pads on that axle are close to worn out,
having them done in the same visit saves paying for the wheel to come off twice.
