## Two batteries, two very different jobs

A hybrid has a small 12 volt battery that runs the lights, locks and
electronics exactly as in any other car, and a separate high voltage traction
battery that drives the motor. When someone says a hybrid needs a new battery,
the first question is which one. The 12 volt battery is an ordinary
replacement at ordinary cost. The traction battery is a major item.

A hybrid that will not wake up is very often a flat 12 volt battery rather
than a failing traction pack, because the 12 volt battery in a hybrid is
usually small and is not charged by an alternator in the conventional way.

## Why hybrid brake pads often last much longer

Hybrids recover energy by using the motor as a generator to slow the car,
which is called regenerative braking. In ordinary driving a large share of the
slowing is done by the motor rather than the friction pads, so pads on a hybrid
frequently outlast those on a comparable conventional car by a wide margin.

This has a consequence that surprises owners: the pads may be barely worn while
the discs have been quietly corroding from light use, particularly in humid
coastal conditions. Low pad wear on a hybrid is normal and is not evidence
that the braking system needs no inspection.

## High voltage systems are not general-purpose work

The traction system runs at voltages that are dangerous to work near, and its
cabling is marked in orange for that reason. Work on it requires specific
training, insulated tools and a documented isolation procedure. A general
garage can service the brakes, tyres, oil and 12 volt system of a hybrid
perfectly well while being the wrong place for the high voltage side.

## Traction battery condition

Traction packs lose capacity gradually rather than failing outright. The usual
signs are the engine running more of the time than it used to, a state of
charge display that swings between full and empty faster than before, and
worse fuel consumption. Individual cell modules can sometimes be replaced or
the pack reconditioned, which is considerably cheaper than a new pack, and
this is worth asking about before accepting a full replacement quote.

## Common hybrids on Sri Lankan roads

Toyota Aqua, Toyota Prius and Honda Vezel are all widely imported and widely
serviced here, so parts availability and familiarity are generally good in the
Colombo area. Fitment still matters: a part listed for one of these will not
necessarily fit another, and grey import variants of the same model name can
differ in specification between markets.
